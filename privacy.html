Parcel Permit Scout: privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026. Applies to the Parcel Permit Scout Chrome extension, version 1.0.0 and later.

In short: everything the extension works with stays in your own browser on your own computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The only websites it contacts are the public permit portals you choose, from your own browser tab, to read their pages.

What the extension handles

  • The spreadsheet you import: parcel numbers, addresses, your notes and any other columns in it.
  • A file of owner names, if you import one.
  • What it reads on the permit portals you choose: the permit and inspection records those sites show to the public, or to you if you are signed in there.
  • Its own settings, activity log and run reports, and the places (portal addresses) you set up.

It does not collect your name, email, browsing history, location, passwords, payment information or anything about websites other than the portals you choose.

Where it is kept

In your browser's storage for this extension, on your computer only. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The extension contains no code loaded from the internet.

What leaves your computer

  • Visits to the permit portals you set up. When you start a check, the extension reads those sites in your own browser tab, the same way you would by searching and clicking yourself. It only searches and reads; a guard refuses anything that would submit, apply, pay, schedule or change something. On a website it does not search by itself (OpenGov towns such as Vail), it reads only the page you have open there, when you press "Read this page". If you are signed in to a portal, it uses your existing session in that tab. It never reads, asks for or stores a password.
  • Nothing else. No information is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Access to each portal is asked for one at a time, with Chrome's own permission prompt. You can remove it at any time.

Files on your computer

Workbooks, CSV files, reports and backups are saved to your Downloads folder when you press a button for them. By default a backup of your readings is also saved there after every run; you can turn that off in Settings. The optional evidence recording (Settings) is time-limited, stays on your computer, and leaves out anything typed into the page.

How your information is used

Only to do what the extension is for: checking the building permits and inspections of the lots in your spreadsheet and showing you the results. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to decide creditworthiness or lending. The use of information received complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting it

In the extension: Clear all results and Remove workbook and results delete what is saved (a backup is offered first). Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it saved. Files you downloaded stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them.

Children

The extension is a work tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date, and the extension's notes for that version say what changed. A change that would send any information off your computer would be asked of you in the extension before it happened.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub, or use the contact email shown on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
